ABOUT US – REGENT FUEL INJECTORS

Regent Fuel Injectors is a trusted name in the automotive industry, backed by over 30 years of hands-on experience. We specialize in diagnostic testing, servicing, and cleaning of automotive gasoline fuel injectors, using advanced technology to move beyond outdated trial-and-error methods. This results in faster, more accurate repairs and consistent performance.

Since 2004, we are constantly upgrading our equipment to stay ahead of evolving industry standards. Our equipment are approved by world-wide leading vehicle manufacturer and injector manufacturers such as BOSCH, Delphi, Magneti Marelli, Hitachi, Denso, Keihin, VDO-Continental, etc allowing us to perform precise and efficient diagnostics on all types of fuel injectors.

With a commitment to precision, reliability, and customer satisfaction, we provide automotive dealers, automotive workshops, vehicle owners, fuel injector service workshops, as well as marine (inboard/outboard) and motorsport vehicles with comprehensive solutions that identify the root cause of fuel injector problems and deliver effective, lasting repairs, ensuring enhanced performance and greater fuel efficiency.

As leaders in the gasoline fuel injector service industry, we pride ourselves on technical expertise, transparent processes, and fast turnaround time, all aimed at delivering fuel injectors renewed to original standards.
