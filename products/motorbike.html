Motorbike Fuel Injectors

High-RPM flow matching, ultrasonic restoration and replacement injectors for performance superbikes, tourers and daily commuters.

Two-Wheeler Precision

High-RPM Motorcycle Fuel Injection

Motorcycle engines operate at significantly higher revolutions per minute (often exceeding 10,000 to 14,000 RPM) compared to standard automotive powerplants. At these engine speeds, the time window for an injector to open, atomize fuel, and snap shut is measured in milliseconds.

Any minute restriction or inconsistent dynamic response produces immediate flat spots in power delivery, harsh throttle snatch, and engine overheating. Regent Fuel Injectors provides calibrated flow balancing, dynamic frequency simulation, and new micro-hardware for single and multi-cylinder two-wheelers.

  • Supported Makes: Yamaha, Honda, Kawasaki, Suzuki, KTM, Royal Enfield, Triumph, Ducati, BMW Motorrad, Harley-Davidson, Bajaj.
  • Superbike Multi-Cylinder Matching: Precise 4-cylinder and twin cylinder flow matching within ±1.0% tolerance.
  • Ultrasonic Scrubbing: Frequency sweep to safely purge ethanol phase-separation gum and varnishes.
