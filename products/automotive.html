Automotive Fuel Injectors

High-precision testing, ultrasonic cleaning, and genuine replacement injectors for passenger cars, SUVs, and commercial vehicles.

Engineering Standards

Passenger Car & Commercial Injection

Automotive fuel injectors must operate reliably under extreme variations in manifold vacuum, fuel pressure, and ambient temperatures. Over thousands of combustion cycles, varnish from heat soak precipitates on microscopic metering nozzles and internal pintles, causing lean misfire, uneven cylinder balance, and sluggish acceleration.

At Regent Fuel Injectors, we service and supply multi-port (MPFI), throttle-body (TBI), side-feed, and direct-injected solenoid injectors. Every unit undergoes multi-angle flow analysis on our BOSCH, Delphi and Magneti Marelli approved test benches.

  • Vehicle Coverage: Maruti Suzuki, Hyundai, Honda, Toyota, Volkswagen, Skoda, BMW, Mercedes-Benz, Ford, Tata, Mahindra.
  • Restoration: New 10–20 micron filter baskets, heat-resistant Viton upper/lower seals and pintle protection caps.
  • Flow Matching: Static and dynamic flow balance guaranteed within ±1.5% across sets.
