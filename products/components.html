Hardware 02

Internal Micro Filter Baskets

Critical 10 to 20 micron stainless steel micro-mesh filter baskets featuring precision brass or plastic crush rings. Stops fuel rail scale, rust and tank debris from reaching the delicate pintle needle orifice. Essential replacement whenever injectors are serviced.

Hardware 03

High-Grade Viton O-Rings

Manufactured from genuine heat-resistant Viton (fluorocarbon elastomer) rated up to 200°C. Highly resistant to gasoline, ethanol blends, motor oil, and fuel pressure surges. Available in standard 14.5mm upper rail diameters, 11mm Japanese diameters, and custom sizes.

Hardware 04

Combustion Chamber Teflon Seals

Specialized PTFE Teflon compression seals for Gasoline Direct Injection (GDI) nozzles and copper manifold crush washers. Engineered to withstand direct exposure to cylinder combustion temperatures and pressures exceeding 2,500 PSI without gas blow-by.

Pintle Caps & Retainer Spacers

Thermal plastic pintle protection caps, electrical harness locking clips, manifold alignment spacers, and fuel rail retaining brackets. Protects delicate needle tips during handling and engine manifold insertion.
