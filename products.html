Product Classification

Major Fuel Injector Categories

Click any category below to access dedicated specifications, detailed photo archives, and custom quotation requests.

Category 01

Automotive Fuel Injectors

Full inventory coverage of MPFI, TBI, Side-Feed and electronic solenoid fuel injectors for Japanese (Maruti, Toyota, Honda), European (Volkswagen, Skoda, BMW, Mercedes), and domestic passenger vehicles and SUVs. Every injector is ultrasonically cleaned, new filters installed, and balanced within 1.5% flow tolerance.

Explore Automotive Range → 10 Real Injector Models Documented
Category 02

Motorbike Fuel Injectors

Engineered for high-RPM operation and precise dynamic pulse widths. Serving single-cylinder daily commuters, cruisers (Royal Enfield, Harley-Davidson), and multi-cylinder superbikes (Yamaha R1/R6, Kawasaki Ninja, Suzuki Hayabusa, Honda CBR). Complete flow reports and resistance matching.

Explore Motorbike Range → 10 Real Injector Models Documented
Category 03

Marine Fuel Injectors (Boat & Jet Ski)

Marine injection systems face severe salt air, fuel moisture, and seasonal storage varnish. We test, clean and supply corrosion-resistant fuel injectors for inboard and outboard boat motors (Mercury, Yamaha Marine, Evinrude) and personal watercraft / jet skis (Sea-Doo, Yamaha WaveRunner, Kawasaki Jet Ski).

Explore Marine Range → 13 Marine & Jet Ski Models Documented
Category 04

Gasoline Direct Injection (GDI)

Operating at ultra-high pressures from 450 to 3,000 PSI, GDI systems demand sub-micron tolerances and specialized flow analysis. We test and supply genuine direct injectors for VW/Audi TFSI, Ford EcoBoost, Hyundai GDI, BMW HPI, Mercedes CGI and Porsche direct injection platforms.

Explore GDI Range → 10 Real GDI Models Documented
Category 05

Injector Components, Seals & Parts

Wholesale and retail supply of vital injector service hardware: 10–20 micron internal filter baskets, fuel-resistant Viton upper and lower O-rings, direct-injection Teflon combustion seals, pintle protection caps, and flow-certified refurbished injectors.
