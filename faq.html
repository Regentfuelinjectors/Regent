Gasoline Direct Injection (GDI) injects a micro-mist directly into the combustion chamber under extreme pressures (450 to 3,000 PSI). Because the tip resides directly in the combustion zone, it accumulates baked carbon crust that distorts spray angle and flow volume. Regular ultrasonic decoking and high-pressure flow testing is critical to avoid lean misfire and engine damage.
95% of fuel injectors do not experience mechanical failure — they are simply clogged by fuel varnish and soot. Replacing working OEM injectors with expensive new units is wasteful. Our 3-stage ultrasonic restoration and micro-filter replacement restores injectors to 100% factory specification for a fraction of the cost of new parts.
