Proven Engineering Results

95% of fuel injectors do not fail. So why replace?

A restriction of just 10% in a single fuel injector is enough to cause a lean misfire. When the oxygen sensor detects excess air, the engine ECU compensates by richening the mixture across all cylinders — degrading mileage, causing hesitation, and building heavy carbon deposits.
